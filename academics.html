Academics

Education at KCC goes beyond academic learning by nurturing students' knowledge, skills, faith, and character in a supportive and Christ-centered environment. Through quality instruction and meaningful learning experiences, we prepare every learner to become a responsible, compassionate, and successful individual ready to face the future.

Basic Education

Nurturing young minds in wisdom, Christian values, and strong character from Kindergarten through Grade 10.

Kindergarten to Grade 6

Elementary Department

The Elementary Department opens its doors to young learners from Kindergarten to Grade 6 in a warm, Christ-centered setting. We pair solid academic instruction with Catholic principles so children flourish both intellectually and spiritually.

Every child is personally known and valued by our educators. Through intimate class sizes, modern learning spaces, and a supportive campus family, each student receives individualized guidance and motivation.

Why Choose KCC Elementary?

  • Catholic teachings woven throughout every subject
  • DepEd-compliant K-12 academic framework
  • Intimate class sizes for tailored instruction
  • Passionate and seasoned teaching staff
  • Secure, caring, and child-friendly campus
  • Consistent spiritual and liturgical activities
  • Diverse clubs, sports, and enrichment programs
  • Active family and parent partnership

Strong Academic Foundation

A well-structured K-6 curriculum meeting DepEd benchmarks while embedding Catholic values across all subjects.

Faith Formation

Frequent Masses, prayer gatherings, and Christian Living lessons that foster spiritual growth from childhood.

Arts & Creativity

Visual arts, music, and stage activities designed to spark imagination and cultural appreciation.

Math & Science

Interactive math and science lessons driven by hands-on experiments and curiosity-based discovery.

Language Proficiency

Focused training in English and Filipino fluency supported by dedicated reading enhancement initiatives.

Character Development

Moral and values formation embedded in daily lessons to shape accountable, empathetic young citizens.

Grades 7 to 10

Junior High School

Spanning Grades 7 through 10, the Junior High School Department expands upon elementary foundations to ready students for Senior High. We deliver the DepEd K-12 curriculum anchored firmly in Catholic faith formation.

Learners actively engage in scholastic contests, athletics, cultural showcases, and outreach programs—experiences that build resilience, confidence, and well-rounded character.

Learning Areas (Grades 7–10)

  • English
  • Mathematics
  • Science
  • Filipino
  • Araling Panlipunan
  • MAPEH (Music, Arts, PE, Health)
  • Edukasyon sa Pagpapakatao (EsP)
  • Technology and Livelihood Education (TLE)
  • Christian Living / Religious Education

Holistic Curriculum

A balanced JHS framework aligned with the K-12 Enhanced Basic Education program and enriched with Catholic values.

STEM Readiness

Rigorous grounding in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics to equip students for Senior High pathways.

Research & Innovation

Foundational training in research methods that cultivates analytical thinking and scientific curiosity.

Co-Curricular Activities

Opportunities in scouting, school journalism, athletics, quiz bees, and division- or regional-level contests.

Spiritual Programs

Recollections, retreats, Eucharistic celebrations, and outreach projects that inspire Christian service.

Student Leadership

Supreme Student Government and classroom councils that nurture leadership, accountability, and civic duty.

Grades 11 & 12

Senior High School

Specialized academic and technical-vocational pathways that prepare learners for higher education, employment, and entrepreneurship.

Charting Your Path to the Future

KCC Senior High School offers both Academic and Technical-Vocational tracks, allowing learners to pursue fields matched to their passions and career ambitions. Every strand integrates Catholic moral formation and holistic student development.
