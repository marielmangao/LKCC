Rooted in Faith, Committed to Excellence

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. is deeply rooted in its Catholic identity and steadfast in upholding the values that define its mission. As an institution, we are committed to providing quality Catholic education that nurtures academic excellence and forms individuals with knowledge, character, and competence, guided by faith and a genuine commitment to serving others. We uphold Catholic education as the very foundation of our identity and the driving force of our mission.

  • Quality Catholic education that nurtures academic excellence.
  • Formation in knowledge, character, and competence, guided by faith.
  • A genuine commitment to serving others and living Catholic values.

Why Choose KCC

For nearly a century, Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. has stood as a pillar of faith-based learning in Southern Negros Occidental, forming graduates who excel professionally and serve their communities with integrity.
